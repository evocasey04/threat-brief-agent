### 2. OpenSSH "sshole" oversized password bypass

**Severity:** high

The flaw allows login to accounts with no password on Alpine Linux and other musl‑based systems by sending an oversized password, granting unauthorized access to privileged accounts. Exploitation can compromise servers exposed to the network, affecting cloud, IoT and container workloads.

**Do this:** Update OpenSSH to the patched version on all Alpine/musl systems or apply vendor backports; enforce password length limits and consider disabling password authentication for vulnerable accounts.
