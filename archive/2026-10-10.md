## Top stories

### 1. GitHub Actions credential‑stealing campaign

**Severity:** high

Compromised maintainer accounts were used to inject malicious workflows into over 340 repositories, exposing credentials and spreading malware to downstream projects. The scale of open‑source supply‑chain impact makes it a high‑risk vector.

**Do this:** Rotate all GitHub tokens, audit recent workflow changes, and enable signed commits for critical repos.

- [The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2026/10/credential-stealing-github-actions.html)

### 2. AhsayCBS backup platform webshells

**Severity:** high

Critical and medium vulnerabilities in the widely deployed AhsayCBS backup solution are being exploited to install webshells and cryptocurrency miners, giving attackers persistent footholds inside enterprise networks.

**Do this:** Apply the vendor's patches immediately; if patches are unavailable, block network traffic to the backup management console and monitor for unknown webshell activity.

- [BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/unpatched-ahsaycbs-flaws-exploited-to-deploy-webshells-mine-crypto/)

### 4. Ad‑network ClickFix attacks

**Severity:** medium

Threat actors abuse Google Ads and Bing redirect URLs to deliver fake Claude installers that install ClickFix malware, leveraging trusted ad platforms to reach end users.

**Do this:** Block execution of installers from ad‑click URLs and educate users to verify digital signatures before running downloads.

- [BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/hackers-abuse-google-ads-bing-redirects-to-push-claude-clickfix-attacks/)
